PulseMesh is a 100% FOSS, zero-infrastructure peer-to-peer communication app for Android and iPhone/Web PWA operating where cellular and internet networks are unavailable, degraded, or untrusted.

Shipped in two GPL-3.0 flavors:
• Full Edition: Native FOSS WebRTC (Opus + AEC3), SQLCipher encrypted Room database, MapLibre Native offline vector maps, and CameraX + ZXing QR scanner.
• Lite Edition: Ultra-compact pure-AOSP build with Android Keystore encryption, MediaCodec Opus/ADPCM voice, Canvas tactical map, and gallery/URI QR pairing.

Key Features in v1.5.5:
• Offline Tactical Map 2.0: MapLibre vector & raster offline maps, MBTiles/PMTiles pack import, bearing/distance ruler, breadcrumbs, GPX/KML import-export, and ATAK Cursor-on-Target (CoT) UDP multicast bridge.
• Camera QR Scanner, NFC Tap-to-Pair & Offline APK Beaming: 1-scan contact add, safety-number verification, group invites, Wi-Fi Hotspot auto-join, NFC HCE pairing, and zero-internet P2P APK beaming with SHA-256 verification.
• LoRa & Acoustic Modem 2.0: USB-OTG serial & BLE GATT companion bridge for Meshtastic and MeshCore radios, plus Bell 202 Acoustic 2.0 with Reed-Solomon GF(2^8) FEC and AES-256-GCM encryption.
• Signal Double Ratchet E2EE & Voice Hub: Forward-secret PMDR2 messaging, zero plaintext downgrades, Android Telecom ConnectionService calling, Zello-style PTT channels, and Biometric/PIN App Lock.
• Modular Web & iPhone PWA: Unified 4-tab UI, Double Ratchet E2EE, Cloudflare Durable Objects WebSocket + TURN, iOS 16.4+ VAPID Web Push, and guided 1-tap iOS .mobileconfig Root CA setup.
• Verified Updater & EN/EL Localization: Fail-closed SHA-256 and signing-certificate verified in-app APK updater with PackageInstaller, plus standard Android strings.xml bilingual EN/EL support.
