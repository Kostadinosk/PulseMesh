PulseMesh is an open-source, zero-infrastructure peer-to-peer communication suite for Android devices operating where cellular and internet networks are unavailable, degraded, or untrusted.

It uses the radios already built into standard Android hardware—Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 1M & Coded PHY), Classic Bluetooth RFCOMM, Wi-Fi Direct & local hotspot subnets, USB/Ethernet tethering, and an Acoustic AFSK audio modem—to discover nearby nodes in under a second, route multi-hop encrypted text and voice traffic, and synchronize GPS waypoints on an offline Web Mercator tile map.

Key Features:
• Multi-Hop DTN Messaging: Broadcast channels, ECDH group chats, and direct messages with TTL=7 flood routing, packet deduplication, delivery ACKs, and a persistent Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN) store-and-forward outbox.
• End-to-End Cryptography: ECDH P-256 / X25519 key agreement, HKDF-SHA256 ratcheting, AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, safety number verification, and encrypted @username key vaults (PMU2 / PMB1).
• Full-Duplex Voice & Walkie-Talkie (PTT): Adaptive 8 kHz G.711 μ-law and 4-bit IMA-ADPCM voice streaming over local Wi-Fi/Hotspot UDP or pure BLE GATT, plus half-duplex echo ducking and voicemail capture.
• Acoustic AFSK Audio Modem: Air-gapped data transmission over phone speakers and microphones (1200 / 2200 Hz Bell 202 tones) with Reed-Solomon forward error correction.
• Offline Tactical Map (EPSG:3857): Regional Web Mercator tile pyramid downloader with local disk caching, shared tactical pins (RALLY, HAZARD, SUPPLY, SOS), live compass bearing HUD, and barometric altitude tracking.
• Strict Off-Grid Mode: When Off-Grid Mode is enabled, all HTTP and external internet connections are hard-blocked at the transport layer.
• 100% Free & Open Source Software (FOSS): Zero proprietary Google Play Services, Firebase, or analytics SDKs.
